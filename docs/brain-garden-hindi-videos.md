# Brain Garden video curation — 15 September 2026

All 211 concepts use one shared video resolver. Fifty verified Hindi videos cover 139 concepts; the other 72 retain English lessons. Hindi is preferred independent of the English/Bengali interface. The original English catalogue remains the fallback. Each Hindi assignment is explicit: a general video is not applied to an entire group by default. Related concepts may share a broader lecture, whose actual title is shown.

Video IDs, authors and embeddability were checked with public YouTube oEmbed and watch-page metadata. Hindi language evidence is the publisher’s explicit Hindi title/channel or an original Hindi caption track. This is a metadata and topic-relevance review, not an independent audit of every spoken claim. Existing English chapter offsets are never carried to a Hindi replacement.

## Fallback policy

Keep the existing English lesson if no suitable, verifiable Hindi replacement was found. Search results about manifestation, guaranteed rapid rewiring, “staying young forever”, or simplistic left/right-brain personality claims were excluded. A neuroplasticity candidate that returned an unavailable oEmbed response was excluded. Availability may change after this check.

The UI labels the video language, shows a thumbnail without creating a player, and embeds only after a click. Every card also provides a direct YouTube link. Changing concept removes the old player so audio cannot continue from a hidden lesson.
